Wellness

Wellness does not need a big overhaul. Small habits repeated on ordinary days, like sleep, water and a few quiet minutes, do most of the work.

Sleep and routines

A steady wake-up time, a screen-free wind-down and a calmer evening, without needing a perfect schedule.

  • Keep a consistent wake-up time, even on weekends, it matters more than a fixed bedtime.
  • Dim lights and step away from screens for 20 to 30 minutes before bed.
  • A short wind-down routine signals your body that it is time to rest.

Hydration

Easy ways to drink more water through the day, from a bottle you like to habits you can attach it to.

  • Keep a bottle you like nearby so drinking water does not need a decision.
  • Pair water with habits you already have, like a glass with each meal.
  • Herbal tea and water-rich fruit count toward daily hydration too.

Simple healthy habits

A short walk, a proper breakfast, stretching at your desk. Habits small enough to stick.

  • A 10-minute walk after a meal can do more than an occasional long workout.
  • Keep one meal a day screen-free so you eat without rushing.
  • Stretch for a few minutes between long stretches of sitting.

Everyday self-care

Low-cost ways to reset after a long day: a warm shower, a tidy space, ten minutes for yourself.
