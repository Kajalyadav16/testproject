Ingredient explainers

Plain-language notes on niacinamide, salicylic acid, hyaluronic acid and more: what each does and who it suits.

  • Niacinamide: calms redness and helps even out skin tone over time.
  • Salicylic acid: unclogs pores, useful for blackheads and bumps.
  • Hyaluronic acid: draws in moisture; suits most skin types.
  • Vitamin C: brightens skin and is best worn under sunscreen in the morning.
