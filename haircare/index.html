Haircare

Healthy hair starts with a scalp that is clean and comfortable, and ends that are kept moisturised. Humidity, hard water and heat styling all play a part.

Frizz care

Why hair frizzes in humid weather, and the small changes in drying, combing and conditioning that help most.

  • Dry hair with a microfibre towel or soft cotton t-shirt instead of rubbing with a regular towel.
  • Apply a leave-in conditioner or a few drops of oil to damp hair, not dry hair.
  • Avoid brushing dry hair too often, it roughs up the cuticle and adds frizz.

Dry hair routines

Gentle wash days, leave-in care and oiling that suit dry, rough or colour-treated hair.

  • Oil your scalp and lengths an hour or two before washing, not right after.
  • Use a sulfate-free shampoo so it cleans without stripping natural oils.
  • Condition every wash, focusing on the ends rather than the scalp.

Hair-wash guides

How often to wash, how much shampoo to use and how to condition properly, based on your scalp type.

  • Most hair types do well with two to three washes a week, not daily.
  • Use lukewarm water, very hot water can dry out scalp and strands.
  • Rinse conditioner thoroughly, leftover product is a common cause of flat, limp hair.
