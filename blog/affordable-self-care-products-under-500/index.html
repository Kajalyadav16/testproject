Affordable Self-Care Products Under ₹500

Wellness, 5 min read

Self-care does not have to be expensive. This guide sorts the essentials from the extras across skin, hair and everyday wellness, and shows how to read labels so a lower price does not mean lower quality.

What this guide covers

  • The few products worth prioritising first
  • How to read ingredient lists without a chemistry degree
  • Where a cheaper option works just as well
  • Signs a product is not right for you
Quick tip: A single multi-use product, like a moisturiser with built-in SPF, can often replace two separate items and save you money.
