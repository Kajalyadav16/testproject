5-Step Beginner Skincare Routine

Skincare, 6 min read

Skincare feels crowded with advice, but a beginner routine can be five short steps. This guide walks through what each step does, the order to use them in, and how to change the routine for oily, dry or sensitive skin.

What this guide covers

  • Cleanse, treat, moisturise and protect, in that order
  • Why sunscreen is the step that matters most in Indian sunlight
  • How to introduce a new product safely with a patch test
  • What to skip until your basics feel comfortable
Quick tip: If you only change one thing today, add a broad-spectrum sunscreen to your morning routine. It does more for long-term skin health than almost anything else.
