Skincare Mistakes Beginners Make

Skincare, 4 min read

Most skin trouble for beginners comes from doing too much, not too little. This guide covers the most common mistakes, why they cause problems, and what to do instead.

What this guide covers

  • Over-exfoliating and damaging your skin barrier
  • Skipping sunscreen on cloudy or indoor days
  • Changing products too often to see results
  • Layering too many actives at once
Quick tip: If a product stings or tingles, that is not proof it is working. It is often a sign to use it less often, not more.
